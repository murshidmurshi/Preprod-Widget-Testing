About Intentick

Intentick is a visitor intent intelligence platform designed to identify, analyze, and engage high-intent visitors on your website in real-time. By tracking on-page buyer signals and behavioural indicators, Intentick empowers marketing and revenue teams to deliver personalized engagement when visitor interest is at its peak.
